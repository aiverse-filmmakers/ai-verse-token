# Deferred Financial-Grade Features

The original research explored invoice reconciliation, account-specific contracts, subscriptions, credit ledgers and other financial-grade behavior. That research remains useful, but these features are outside the first release.

They may be introduced later as additive modules if real user demand justifies them. They must not complicate or weaken the first-release usage ledger, collectors, timing, pricing snapshots, cost statuses or AI-Verse integration.

Deferred capabilities include:

- invoice/billing reconciliation;
- account-specific contract tariffs;
- subscription and credit settlement;
- multi-currency financial accounting;
- billing variance/dispute workflows.

The first release uses only `ACTUAL`, `CALCULATED` and `UNKNOWN` monetary states.
